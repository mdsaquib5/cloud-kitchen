# ⚠️ Risk Register & Mitigation Playbook

| # | Risk Area | Ground Reality | Severity | Concrete Mitigation Strategy |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :-: | :--- |
| **1** | **Demand Generation Deficit** | Software cannot generate demand for a zero-reputation cloud kitchen. Direct channels make ₹0 without existing footfall. | **CRITICAL** | • Strict client selection: Target restaurants with existing footfall, phone ordering habits, or bakery/tiffin clientele.<br/>• Set up Google Business Profile "Order Online" links on day 1.<br/>• Enforce delivery bag flyers & QR table standees with 15% first direct order incentives. |
| **2** | **Crowded Competitor Field** | Petpooja (45k+), DotPe, uEngage, Thrive, plus free trial tools sell a similar pitch. | **HIGH** | • Do not compete on feature count; compete on **physical locality, high-touch support, and instant turnaround**.<br/>• Be in their kitchen within 20 minutes for hardware/menu issues.<br/>• Handle their menu import and WhatsApp marketing end-to-end. |
| **3** | **Aggregator Retaliation** | Incumbents may penalize ranking, reduce visibility, or enforce exclusivity. | **HIGH** | • Position our product as a **hybrid retention channel**, not an aggressive aggregator boycott.<br/>• Encourage restaurants to use Swiggy/Zomato as a customer acquisition funnel and direct channels for repeat orders. |
| **4** | **High Restaurant Churn** | 25–35% of new cloud kitchens fail within 12 months due to high rentals and food waste. | **HIGH** | • Qualify restaurants with at least 6–12 months of operating history.<br/>• Charge upfront onboarding/setup fees (₹3,000–5,000) to filter tire-kickers.<br/>• Focus retention telemetry on 30-day repeat customer rate. |
| **5** | **24×7 Operational Support Load** | Kitchen software cannot fail during Friday–Sunday 8–10 PM dinner rush. | **HIGH** | • Implement automated unaccepted order escalation calls.<br/>• Build auto-recovering WebSocket connections with fallback polling.<br/>• Set up automated alerting (PagerDuty/Telegram bots) for server error rates. |
| **6** | **Legal & Regulatory (GST / PA)** | Centralized money collection incurs RBI Payment Aggregator licensing rules and CGST Sec 9(5) liability. | **CRITICAL** | • Strictly operate under Model A (White-label SaaS) with direct merchant payment gateways.<br/>• Money flows directly from customer to restaurant; platform invoices monthly subscription or uses authorized split settlements. |
